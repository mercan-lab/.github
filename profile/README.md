### 🪸 Neden "Mercan"?

Mercan resifleri, kalsiyum karbonat çöktüren sayısız küçük canlının bir araya gelerek
yüzyıllarca ayakta kalan yapılar kurmasıyla oluşur. Bu süreç doğadaki en eski
**biyomineralizasyon** örneklerinden biridir ve ilk projemizin betonda incelediği süreçle
aynıdır.

Biz de böyle çalışıyoruz: **dört üniversiteden ve dört farklı disiplinden** öğrenciler,
ortak bir yapıya her biri kendi parçasını ekliyor.

## 🪸 Why "Mercan"?

*Mercan* is Turkish for **coral**. A coral reef is built by countless small organisms that
precipitate calcium carbonate and, together, raise structures that last for centuries.
It is also one of nature's oldest examples of **biomineralization**, the same process our
first project studies in concrete.

We work the same way: a small group of students from **four universities and four
disciplines**, each adding one piece to a shared structure.

## 🔬 What we do

We run data-driven research at the intersection of fields that rarely sit at the same table:

| | Area | What it brings |
|:-:|---|---|
| 🏗️ | **Construction materials** | Engineering questions, performance criteria, real-world use cases |
| 🧬 | **Biology** | Organisms, mechanisms and biosafety |
| 💻 | **Computing** | Data pipelines, statistics and machine learning |
| ✍️ | **Language** | Terminology, annotation guidelines and scientific writing |

## 🧫 Current project

<table>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <b>Bacteria for self-healing concrete (MICP)</b><br><br>
      Some bacteria can precipitate calcium carbonate and seal cracks in concrete.
      The literature on this process is scattered across many species and lab conditions,
      which makes results hard to compare. We are building an <b>open, normalized dataset</b>
      that links reported concrete performance to bacterial strains and experimental conditions.
    </td>
  </tr>

## 🧭 How we work

- **Open by default.** Data, code and methods are published once a study is ready.
- **Reproducible.** Every number in our outputs traces back to a source file or a script.
- **Human-verified AI.** We use AI assistants for reading and coding support; nothing enters our data or code without a human check, and we disclose AI use in our papers.
- **Careful claims.** We write "to our knowledge" rather than "the first".
